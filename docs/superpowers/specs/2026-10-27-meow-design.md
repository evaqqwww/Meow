After an interrupted hold's reverse completes, preserve the submitted input and allow a fresh attempt; do not allow another hold during reverse playback. After a successful hold, wait for release and reverse completion (and the 1000 ms reply period) before returning to idle and allowing a new message. If reverse playback ends first, keep the cat on frame 0 until the blessing has been visible for the full 1000 ms. The interaction can be repeated.
